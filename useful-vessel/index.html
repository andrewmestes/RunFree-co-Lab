Good questions

Before you call

How much do you charge?

Most jobs are quoted as a flat price so you know the number before we start. For longer lists, a half-day or full-day rate is usually the better deal. Either way, you'll have the price in writing before any work begins — and it doesn't change unless you add to the list.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Describe the job over the phone or send a couple of photos and we can usually quote it on the spot. If it needs an in-person look, that visit is free too.

Are you licensed and insured?

We carry liability insurance on every job, and we're happy to send proof before we start. For work that legally requires a licensed specialty trade — major electrical panels, gas lines, structural changes — we'll tell you straight and refer you to a licensed pro we trust rather than take the job.

Is there a minimum job size?

There's a minimum service charge that covers travel and the first stretch of work — so if you've got several small items, group them into one visit. It's the same trip for us and a much better value for you.

How soon can you get here?

Most jobs get scheduled within the same week. If it's urgent — a leak, a door that won't lock, something unsafe — say so when you call and we'll do our best to work you in sooner.

Do you take on big remodels?

We stay in our lane. Repairs, replacements, installs, and small projects are what we do well. Full kitchen and bath gut-jobs or additions aren't our thing — and we'd rather tell you that than learn on your house.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Tell us before we leave, or call us after. We stand behind what we do and we'll come back and make it right. Our reputation in this town is worth more than any single invoice.

How do I pay?

Payment is due when the work is finished and you're satisfied with it. We accept cash, check, and major cards.
