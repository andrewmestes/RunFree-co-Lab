Why a church for seniors?

The biggest wave in America's history is grey. Almost nobody is planting churches for it.

Every day, about 10,000 Americans turn 65. By 2030, every Baby Boomer will be past that line. It is the largest, fastest-growing group of people in the country, and in Kankakee County it is your neighbors, your card club, your coffee crowd.

Meanwhile, nearly every new church is designed for young families, with the lights, the volume, and the pace to match. Older adults get a "senior ministry" on Tuesday morning, if they get anything. Many quietly stop coming. Many more never started.

Grey Wave is not a program inside a church. It is a church. Built for you, led with you, and aimed at the people in your life who think God is finished with them. He is not.

What to expect

Church the way it should feel at this stage of life.

Simple, warm, and unhurried. Here is what a Sunday looks like.

Songs you already know

Hymns and the worship songs of the last fifty years, sung at a pitch and a volume you can actually join. Words in large print, always.

A sermon you can follow

One passage of the Bible, explained plainly, applied to real life: grief, grandkids, health, money, regret, and what comes next. About 25 minutes. Lance has been doing this for 45 years.

Communion every week

We gather around the Lord's table every Sunday, the way the first Christians did. Everyone who belongs to Jesus is welcome at it.

Coffee and a real conversation

We start early with coffee and we stay late. Nobody sits alone, and nobody gets rushed out the door to make room for the next service.

Daytime, one hour, done

No driving after dark. No two-hour marathons. Level entrance, comfortable seats, good sound, and a restroom you don't need a map for.

Come as you are

Church background or none. Baptist, Catholic, Lutheran, lapsed, or "spiritual but done with religion." Bring your questions. Bring your walker. Bring your friend.

What we believe

Christians only, but not the only Christians.

Grey Wave is an independent Christian church in the Restoration Movement tradition, the same family as Ignite Church Planting, Lincoln Christian University, and hundreds of Christian churches across Illinois and Indiana. Our aim has been the same for two hundred years: get back to the simple church of the New Testament.

  • Jesus alone is Savior and Lord. Not a denomination, not a preacher, not a program.
  • The Bible alone is our authority. Where the Scriptures speak, we speak. Where they are silent, we leave room.
  • No creed but Christ. We ask people to trust and follow Jesus, not to sign a statement.
  • Baptism by immersion, for people old enough to choose it, as the New Testament describes. It is never too late. Lance has baptized people in their 80s.
  • The Lord's Supper every Lord's Day. Communion is the center of our Sunday, not an occasional add-on.
  • A self-governing congregation, led by elders from among our own people and answerable to no headquarters.
"In essentials, unity. In non-essentials, liberty. In all things, love."A Restoration Movement motto since the early 1800s
What we care about

Four things you will see us do over and over.

Prayer first

Ignite's first value is prayer, and it is ours. Before we plan, we pray. Lance and Darla keep a running list of names, and yours can be on it.

Nobody sits alone

Loneliness is the quiet epidemic of later life. We will know your name, notice when you are missing, and show up when the doctor's news is bad.

Still growing

Retirement is not a spiritual finish line. We expect people in their 70s to learn, serve, lead, and be baptized. God is not done with you, so neither are we.

Finish well, pass it on

You carry sixty years of stories, skills, and faith. This church exists to put them to work, in the lives of your grandchildren and in the next Grey Wave church.

Our pastor

Meet Lance Hurley.

Lance has spent 45 years starting churches in Chicagoland, and he is not done. Grey Wave is the church he has wanted to plant for a long time: one for his own generation.

Lance grew up in Ocala, Florida, in a ministry home; his father and brother both preach. He graduated from Lincoln Christian College in 1981 with a preaching degree, and before he had his diploma in hand he was asked to plant a brand-new church in Manteno, Illinois. He said yes. He was 22.

Nineteen years later, the Christian Church of Manteno had grown from a core group of 20 to 250 people, and more than 400 had been baptized. After pastoring two churches in Florida, Lance came home to plant Cornerstone Christian Church in Glen Ellyn in 2005. Today he serves as Executive Director of Ignite Church Planting, the hundred-year-old network that has started or helped start 40 churches across Chicagoland since 2001.

Lance and Darla have been married 42 years and live in Bourbonnais. Darla keeps Ignite running from the office; Lance keeps it running from the road. They have watched a lot of friends drift away from church in retirement, and they decided to do something about it.

Get involved

Five ways to be part of the wave.

Come

Sunday is the front door. Just show up. We will save you a seat and a cup of coffee.

Bring someone

Think of one person who used to go to church and stopped. Ask them. That is how nearly everyone here arrived.

Pray

Join the prayer list and get one email a week with names and needs. Retired people have the time to pray, and it shows.

Serve

Greet, make coffee, drive a neighbor, visit a shut-in, run the sound board. Sixty years of skills do not retire.

Give

New churches are funded by the people who believe in them. Gifts through Ignite Church Planting are tax-deductible. Ask Lance how.

For pastors and partners

One church is the start. The wave is the point.

If Grey Wave works in Bourbonnais, it can work in every county with a Walmart and a senior center. We are documenting everything so the next one is easier to plant than this one.

Grey Wave is a project of Ignite Church Planting, whose vision is ten church-planting clusters across Chicagoland, each producing a new church every three years. We would love to add "a church for seniors" to every cluster's list. If you lead a church with a large, faithful, aging congregation and a small budget line for them, let's talk.
