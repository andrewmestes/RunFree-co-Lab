Connect

Sunday is the front door. Here's the rest of the house.

Groups to belong to, teams to serve on, and people who will pray with you and show up when it counts.

Groups

Find your people.

Small, weekday, daytime, and built around a table. Most groups run six to eight weeks so it's easy to try one.

Weekday Bible Study

Wednesdays, 10:00 am. One chapter a week, large-print Bibles provided, and no question is a dumb question.

Coffee & Conversation

Tuesdays, 10:00 am. No agenda. Coffee, a table, and whoever shows up. The easiest first step there is.

Grief Group

Six weeks, starting Oct 7. For anyone who has lost a spouse, a child, or a close friend. Led by people who've walked it.

Men's Breakfast & Women's Coffee

Once a month, at a local restaurant. Come hungry. Dates on the events page.

Serve

Sixty years of skills don't retire.

A church this size runs on its people. Pick something that fits and we'll show you the ropes.

  • Greeters: meet people at the door so nobody walks in alone
  • Coffee team: the most important ministry in the building
  • Ride team: pick up neighbors who can't drive
  • Visitation: hospitals, rehab, homebound friends
  • Sound & screen: run the words and the microphones
  • Prayer team: pray through the list every week
  • Meals: cook for a family in a hard season
  • Handyman crew: fix what's broken, for us and for neighbors

Care

When life gets hard, you won't face it alone.

Hospital stays, surgeries, a spouse in memory care, a diagnosis you're still absorbing. Tell us. Someone will come.

  • Hospital and home visits
  • Meals in a hard season
  • Rides to church, appointments, and the pharmacy
  • A phone call when you've been missing
  • Funeral and memorial support for families
